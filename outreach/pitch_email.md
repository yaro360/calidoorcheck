## CAN-SPAM basics

Commercial email needs, at minimum:

- Accurate From / Subject lines (no bait-and-switch).
- A clear identification that this is a business email about a paid listing.
- A physical postal mailing address in the email body.
- A working way to opt out, honored promptly (the "Reply no" line below). Keep a record of opt-outs and do not email those companies again.
