This Old House lists garage door opener replacement at $430–$730 and automatic opener installation at $500–$860 (national, Homewyse data, updated March 2026).

Compare quotes on the same thing

  • Ask for the opener brand and model on the estimate so you can compare like-for-like.
  • Ask whether removal and disposal of the old opener is included.
  • Ask whether you can supply your own opener and pay installation only, and get that price in writing.

Before you approve

Openers are a common upsell during spring repairs. If the old opener works, you can decline and decide later. Check the license first.
