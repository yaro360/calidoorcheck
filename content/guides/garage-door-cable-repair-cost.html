Cables and springs often go together

Cables and springs work as a system, so a technician replacing springs may also recommend cables. That can be legitimate — but ask to see the frayed or broken cable, and ask for the cable work as a separate line item on a written estimate.

Before you approve

  • Confirm the company's CSLB D-28 license.
  • Compare against spring replacement ranges if springs are bundled in.
  • Get a second quote if the total is far above these ranges.
