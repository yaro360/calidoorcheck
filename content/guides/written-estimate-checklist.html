Both homeowners in ABC7's September 2026 garage door story agreed to work without a written estimate. Use this list before you say yes.

The estimate should show

  • The contractor's state license number (CSLB requires it on bids and contracts) and a business name that matches the license.
  • A business address and phone number (CSLB advises verifying both).
  • Itemized parts and labor — each spring, cable, roller or opener as its own line.
  • Whether prices are per spring or per pair.
  • Information about workers' compensation and general liability insurance (CSLB says a licensed contractor must provide both in your written contract).

Payment rules to know

Per CSLB, on home improvement projects a contractor generally cannot ask for a down payment of more than 10% of the contract price or $1,000, whichever is less, unless they hold a rare blanket performance and payment bond on file with CSLB.

Then

Verify the license, compare with published price ranges, and get another written bid for anything large.
