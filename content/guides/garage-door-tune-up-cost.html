Why a much cheaper tune-up ad deserves caution

ABC7 reported in September 2026 that a Woodland Hills homeowner responded to a text ad for a $97 tune-up and said she ended up paying about $4,000. A tune-up priced below the published range can be a loss-leader to get a technician to your door.

Protect yourself

  • Ask what the tune-up includes, in writing, before the visit.
  • If the tech finds "urgent" problems, ask for an itemized written estimate and photos of the worn parts.
  • Get a second quote for anything beyond the tune-up. See the scam playbook and how to check a license.
