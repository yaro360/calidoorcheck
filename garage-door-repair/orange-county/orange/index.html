How many licensed garage door companies are in Orange?

26 hold an active CSLB D-28 (Doors, Gates & Activating Devices) license with a business address in Orange, Orange County, as of the CSLB public list downloaded 2026-11-22. Licenses change daily; confirm on CSLB Instant License Check.

How do I verify a garage door contractor's license in Orange?

Ask for the California contractor license number before anyone comes out, then look it up on CSLB Instant License Check. Confirm the business name matches, the status is current, and the classifications include D-28. See how to check a license in 60 seconds.

Does a D-28 license mean the company only repairs garage doors?

No. CSLB's D-28 classification covers installing, modifying, or repairing residential, commercial, or industrial doors, including overhead or sliding door assemblies, and also covers gates and activating devices. A business address in this city is also not a service area.

How many Orange D-28 license holders have workers' compensation insurance on file?

Of the 26 active D-28 license holders with a business address in Orange, 15 show a workers' compensation insurance policy on file and 10 are marked exempt (no employees declared), according to CSLB data downloaded 2026-11-22.
