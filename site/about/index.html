About CaliDoorCheck

Last updated 2026-11-22. Company listings come from the CSLB public List by Classification and County (C-61/D-28), downloaded 2026-11-22. Licenses change daily; always confirm on CSLB Instant License Check.

CaliDoorCheck helps Southern California homeowners verify a garage door company before they call. The directory currently covers Los Angeles, Orange, Ventura, Riverside, San Bernardino, and San Diego counties.

Where the company data comes from

Every listing comes from the California Contractors State License Board's free List by Classification and County download for classification C-61/D-28 (Doors, Gates & Activating Devices), which contains currently renewed licenses. Last downloaded 2026-11-22. We show license number, business name, city, ZIP, phone, classifications, dates, bond surety and workers' comp status as published by CSLB. We do not show street addresses on listing pages.

What we don't do

  • We don't invent prices; price pages cite their sources.
  • We don't rank companies by payment. Any future paid placement will be labeled "Sponsored" and limited to CSLB-licensed companies.
  • We don't publish reviews or ratings.
