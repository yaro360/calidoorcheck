How do I verify a garage door contractor's license in Canoga Park?

Ask for the California contractor license number before anyone comes out, then look it up on CSLB Instant License Check. Confirm the business name matches, the status is current, and the classifications include D-28. See how to check a license in 60 seconds.

Does a D-28 license mean the company only repairs garage doors?

No. CSLB's D-28 classification covers installing, modifying, or repairing residential, commercial, or industrial doors, including overhead or sliding door assemblies, and also covers gates and activating devices. A business address in this city is also not a service area.
