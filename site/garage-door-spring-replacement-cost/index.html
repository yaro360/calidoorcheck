How to read a quote

  • Pair vs single: This Old House notes pros usually recommend replacing both springs together, and its prices assume a set. Ask whether the quote is per spring or for the pair.
  • Torsion vs extension: torsion springs (on a bar above the door) cost more and are more dangerous to DIY; extension springs (along the tracks) cost less.
  • Add-ons: This Old House lists cable repair at $170–$320 and a tune-up at $120–$240. If cables, drums and rollers all get added, ask to see the worn parts.
  • Emergency fees: after-hours and same-day service can cost more.

When to stop and get a second quote

If a spring job is heading into the thousands, pause. In the ABC7 case a $97 tune-up ad ended in a bill of about $4,000. Ask for an itemized written estimate and check the license before you sign.
